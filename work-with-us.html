Work with us

Bring the apocalypse to you

We’re a road-tested touring show with a loyal following, a two-hour narrative format and a set that fits in the back of a car. Venues, festivals, programmers and fellow improvisers — let’s talk.

Venues & festivals

We’ve played studio theatres, pubs, community venues and fringe festivals across Bristol and London — from the Tobacco Factory to the Camden Fringe. The show runs around two hours with an interval and needs very little tech: a few lights, a few chairs, and an audience willing to write one thing down.

Private & corporate events

Team away-day? Birthday? Company Christmas party that’s bored of quizzes? We can build a story around your suggestions, your in-jokes or your CEO’s worst habits. Runs from 45 minutes to a full evening.

Workshops & collaborations

Narrative improv, world-building, genre play and the fine art of committing to a bad accent. We run sessions for improv groups and community organisations, and we’re always up for double-bills with other shows.
